Before you join

  • On a phone, install the free Google Meet app first, then tap Join.
  • Keep your mic muted unless you are speaking, to avoid noise and echo.
  • The meeting may end automatically after about 60 minutes. If it does, tap Join the Service again to return.
  • Use Wi-Fi if you can. Turn your camera off if your connection is slow.
